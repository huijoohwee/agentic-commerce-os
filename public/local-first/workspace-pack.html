What happens to my code?
After your review, the source is sent to this service for conversion. It is not executed or stored. The generated files stay in this page until you download them or leave.
Can I use it offline?
Conversion needs a connection to this service. Downloaded files can be kept and edited locally. This page does not save an unfinished source across reloads.
Use this service from an agent
REST, MCP and browser WebMCP use the same conversion. Agents provide the exact source and its SHA-256 digest; the browser review applies to requests made with this form.
